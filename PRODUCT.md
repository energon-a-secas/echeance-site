# Echeance: product one-pager

**What it is:** Track token expiry and the renewal steps for each

**Who it is for:** A developer running a fleet of small services on a pile of
API keys, OAuth sessions and allowlists, who discovers each expiry the day a
deploy fails and by then has forgotten how the credential was obtained.

**Register:** product (a tool someone uses), not brand (a page someone visits).

**The scene:** A terminal error says auth failed; the person opens Echeance in
the next tab, sees which credential died and when, and follows its tutorial
without leaving the chair. Dark, dense, built for a quick consult mid-incident.

**What done looks like:** The person knows what expires next, has the renewal
steps in front of them, and their calendar carries the alarm for next time.
